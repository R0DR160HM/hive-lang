# v0.3.9

## Tooling

* **`hive install <entrypoint.hive>` builds a program and puts it where a shell finds commands**: `~/.local/bin` on Linux and macOS, and on Windows `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Hive\bin`, put on the user's `PATH` the first time. `--name` installs it under another name.
* **`hive install --desktop` adds a windowed program to the system's app launcher**, with its icon, on Linux, macOS and Windows.
* **`hive install --target android/arm64` installs the program as an app on a phone connected over USB**, through `adb`.

## The terminal

* **`hive.term.print` ends no line, and `hive.term.println` is the one that does**, so a prompt or an escape sequence is written as it is.
* **A terminal is read a key at a time**: `raw` stops the echo and the line buffering, `readKey` answers each key named as `hive.ui` names it and a paste as one, and `size` measures the window.
* **Raw mode is put back however a program ends**: `main` returning, a panic, `hive.term.exit`, or its last window closing.
* **A Windows console paints the escape sequences a program prints**, as every other terminal does.
* **`hive.term.start` runs a program alongside this one**: its output read while it runs, its input written, and `processKill` stopping it with everything it started; whatever is left is stopped when this program ends.

## Command-line flags

* **`hive.term.flags()` is the program's arguments as one line `hive.term.codec()` reads back word for word**, a word holding a space or a quote quoted again; `join(hive.term.args(), " ")` split such a word in two.
* **A flag takes a short name**: `-- Flag as background-task bg;` reads `-bg` as well as `--background-task`, the shorter of the two names being the short one.
* **`hive.term.positional(line)` answers with a command line's own words**: those before the first flag, and everything after a lone `--`.
* **The flag codec pairs words knowing the type**: a `Bool` takes the next word only when it is `true` or `false`, and a negative number is a value, never a flag.

## Files, pictures and HTTP

* **`hive.net.httpOpen` reads a response as it arrives**: it answers once the headers are in, `httpReceiveLine` and `httpReceive` hand the body over piece by piece, and `httpClose` drops the connection, which is what stops a model generating.
* **`hive.image` reads PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP pictures** into their pixels, and `scale` resizes one.
* **`hive.file` says where the program is**: `workingDirectory`, `home`, `executable`, and `isDir` beside `exists`.
* **`hive.file.asset` reads the program's own `assets/` folder out of its executable**, which is built in whenever a program reads it, window or none.

## Windows and pages

* **`hive.ui` lays out grids and stacks**: `grid` with `columns` and `span`, `stack` with `place`, a row that `wrap`s, and `minWidth`, `maxWidth`, `minHeight`, `maxHeight` and `aspect` on any box.
* **Text takes a weight, a slant and a length**: `weight`, `italic`, `mono`, `lines` ending in an ellipsis, `justify` across a text's lines, and `paragraph` for words in several styles wrapped as one.
* **`radio`, `choices`, `tabs`, `tree` and `progress` are widgets**, the first four heard through `onChoose`, `on` and `onToggle`.
* **Any widget hears the pointer and the focus**: `onEnter`, `onLeave`, `onPress`, `onPoint`, `onRelease`, `onWheel`, `onFocus`, `onBlur`, `onScroll` and `onSize`; `carry` and `onDrop` drag one onto another, and `onFile` hears a file chosen or dropped.
* **A box looks more ways**: `gradient`, `opacity`, `shadow`, `ease` to bring a change in over time, and `fit` for a picture.
* **`limits` sets a slider's or a number's range and step**, and `InputKind` adds `Time`, `DateTime`, `Email`, `Tel`, `Url`, `Color` and `File`.
* **`Icon` has 320**, drawn from Lucide alike on a page and in a window.
* **A canvas draws more**: `oval`, `arc`, `wedge`, `curve`, `cutout`, `symbol`, `picture`, an `edge`, a `dash` and a `gradient`, and `measure` says how wide `words` will be everywhere.

## Scenes

* **A scene has more shapes**: `torus`, `capsule`, `disc`, `mesh`, `tube`, `extrude`, `lathe`, `cloud`, `model`, `sign`, `group`, and a `line` with `thick`.
* **A shape says how it shines**: `rough`, `metal`, `glow`, `unlit`, `wire`, `shadows`, and a `texture` and `relief` of its own laid by `tile`; `scale` sizes every shape.
* **A scene's light is the program's**: `lamp`, `spot`, `sunlight`, `ambient`, a `panorama`, and a `background` with no alpha to see through.
* **The camera can `orbit`, look `toward` a place, `roll`, go `ortho` and set its `depth`.**
* **A scene says what is under the pointer** through `onHit` and `onOver`, and hands over a PNG of itself through `snapshot` and `onSnapshot`.
* **A `model` or `figure` plays its file's animations** with `play(clip, at, loop)`, and a glTF without a skin, or with many meshes, is drawn whole.

## Fixes

* **`onDrag` reaches a scene on a page**, where the picture drawn over the scene took the press.
* **Writing an element inside a branch or a loop keeps what was proved about its vector**: `t[k] = 1` in an inner `if` cost the outer `t bounds k`, which a write cannot change.
* **A proof is lost when what it was about is replaced**: an `indexOf` over a vector since rebound, and a guard on `grid[0]` after `grid[0] = [1]`, still proved an index that could be out of range.

## Breaking

| was | is |
| --- | --- |
| an index proved for a vector, or for a row, used after the vector or a row of it is written | guarded again after the write: writing `grid[1]` costs the proofs about every row of `grid` |
| `hive.term.print(text)` ending its line | `hive.term.println(text)` |
| `--verbose file.txt`, an error about `file.txt` | `file.txt` is a word, and words go before the flags or after `--` |
| a flag the type does not declare, or a word between the flags, passed over | a `FlagError` |
| a value opening with a dash, `--name -x` | `--name=-x`, or quoted |
