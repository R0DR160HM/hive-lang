# v0.3.5

## Standard library

* **A `scene` is drawn by [Filament](https://github.com/google/filament)**, through an engine of Hive's own in `scene/`, and three.js is gone.
* **Windows and Linux draw a window with a `scene` in it themselves**, on amd64 (Linux with glibc 2.38 or newer): the engine runs beside the program, on Vulkan or OpenGL, and no browser opens.
* **A page draws with the same engine, as WebAssembly**, so a scene looks the same wherever it is shown.
* **`hive.audio` plays in a window that draws its own scene**, through the scene's engine: an MP3 stream, or a recording in MP3, Ogg Vorbis, FLAC or WAV.
* **A `row` or `column` wider than the row it sits in is cut to it in a window that draws itself**, as it always was on a page.

## Tooling

* **`scene/build`** builds the engine; a release's compiler fetches its own against pinned digests, and `HIVE_SCENE_DIST` names a directory to take it from instead.
